How it works

Two layers of checks. First, fast pattern rules look for common warning signs: shortened or lookalike links, pressure to act quickly, and requests for gift cards, crypto or transfers. Then an AI model reads the whole message for anything the rules can't spot, like someone pretending to be a family member. If the AI is unavailable, you still get the rule-based result.

It never opens links in your message, and it never stores what you paste. It can't prove a message is safe, only point out warning signs, so if something feels wrong, trust your instincts.
