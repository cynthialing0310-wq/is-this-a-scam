Is This a Scam?

Paste a suspicious text message, email or marketplace listing, and get a calm, plain-English check of the warning signs.

What it will tell you

  • A risk level: Likely scam, Suspicious, or No obvious red flags.
  • The red flags it found, each linked to the exact words that triggered it.
  • What to do next, such as not clicking a link and contacting the company through its official website.
  • A note when it isn't sure, instead of guessing.

How it works

Two layers of checks. Fast rule-based checks look for known warning signs, like shortened links, pressure to act quickly, and requests for gift cards or crypto. An AI model then reads the whole message. If the AI is unavailable, you still get the rule-based result.

It never opens links in your message, and it never stores what you paste.

Built with

Python, FastAPI, the Claude API, Pydantic and pytest.
